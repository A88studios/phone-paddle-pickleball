Phone Paddle Pickleball

Your phone is the paddle. Stand on the court and swing.

  1. Your phone is the paddle. Hold it at a comfortable angle, like reading a text, and you see straight down the court through a see-through paddle face. Turn or tilt the phone to look around and follow the ball.
  2. Drop it down and turn it to your side like a real paddle and you see its edge. Swing your hand through the ball when it glows yellow.
  3. Placement: swing early to pull the ball across, late to push it the other way, and point the paddle face where you want it to go. Swing harder to hit deeper.
  4. Height: your normal angle hits a low drive. Tilt the face back for a floater, way back to pop up a lob.
  5. Rally scoring to 11, win by 2. The serve and the return must bounce before they're hit.
