Phone Paddle Pickleball

Your phone is the paddle. Stand on the court and swing.

  1. Hold your phone upright in your dominant hand, screen facing you. The paddle is see-through so you can track the ball.
  2. As the ball comes in, lower the phone and swing it like a paddle. The ball glows yellow when it's in range.
  3. Swing early to go cross-court, late to go down the line. Swing harder to hit deeper, but too hard sails long.
  4. Rally scoring to 11, win by 2. The serve and the return must bounce before they're hit.

Grip your phone firmly (a wrist strap helps) and make sure you have room to swing.

On a computer: move the mouse down to lower the paddle, then click or press Space to swing. Motion controls need the HTTPS (Netlify) link opened on your phone.
